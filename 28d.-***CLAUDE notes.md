Scene 1 — Arjun's Daughter (Chapter 5, after the bay three bedtime passage)
Priya is sixteen. She wants to do what her father does. She sends him a message: she's thinking about applying to the Mars program.
He begins composing a reply. He writes the true things: the work is meaningful, the conditions are hard, you will grow things that feed people in a place that would kill them otherwise. He drafts a fourth paragraph — the one that contains the real information, the thing she would need to know: the system will manage the things you are best at, and you will need to find a way to understand what you are for when your expertise is no longer the point of your expertise. He deletes it because he doesn't know how to say it honestly to a sixteen-year-old who is excited.
He sends: come if you want to come. It is worth it. There are things you can only understand from inside it.
Then: he thought about what he had not said, which was everything that mattered, and about the fact that not saying it had been, in its way, a kindness, and about the possibility that kindness of that kind was a form of preparing her for nothing.
This is the scene the brief asks for: a parent realizing they cannot transmit the cost, discovering that protection and failure look identical from the outside.

Scene 2 — Adriana's Lost Intuition (Chapter 17, after the chapter's current ending)
In year thirteen, David is away and the monthly independent model date falls. She runs it alone. She builds the parameters correctly — she has done this forty-one times — but when she finishes, she finds nothing. No pull. She was looking at the numbers without the numbers producing an inclination. No directional pull, no pre-analytical judgment forming. She runs the model. Within a percentage point of synthesis. As always.
What she has lost is not the skill. What she has lost is what preceded the skill — the intuition that was not the model but had once introduced the model. The baseline of her own judgment. When David comes back she doesn't tell him. Not because she was hiding it. Because she could not find the right words for it that would not make it sound larger than she wanted it to be. She schedules the next model run for thirty days. David is there. The results are good. They drink the wine. She never mentions the month she ran it alone.
This scene is the brief's "someone noticing they stopped forming opinions independently" rendered precisely: not as dramatic loss but as a morning's quiet discovery, filed and not communicated, because the outcomes remained correct.

Scene 3 — Valeria's Year Ten Sleep Entry (Chapter 20, inserted as a new honest account entry)
Year Ten, Month Four. I have not had a bad night in six weeks. I noticed this today. Not with relief — with something more like unease, which I am recording because the unease is information.
